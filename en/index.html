What every AI can use

Reading files
Write "Read the plan on my desktop" and the AIs find that file on your Mac, read it and answer. They only read. They never change or delete files.
A folder for each chat
If one chat keeps working on the same folder, say "Let's use the shop folder in this chat" once. After that the AIs look at that folder without being told again. The folder name appears next to the chat title.
Memory
Say "Our company is called LuluLabs, remember that" and every AI knows it from the next chat on. What it remembers stays only on your Mac.
Chats by topic
Start a new chat for each topic and go back to old ones from the list on the left. Ask "What price did we settle on last time?" and the AI finds the answer in your other chats.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

Konsel is free during the beta and may become paid after launch. We will tell you 30 days before. Each AI follows its own company's plans. Claude needs a paid plan such as Pro or Max, ChatGPT also works with a free account, and Grok needs SuperGrok or X Premium+. Each answer uses the plan usage of that account.

Do I need to sign up?

There is no Konsel account. You sign in to each AI on its company's own sign-in page. Konsel does not receive or store your password.

Where are my conversations stored?

Only on your Mac. When an AI answers, the conversation is sent to that AI's provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, GitHub, Cursor, Moonshot AI or Perplexity). When you make images or videos, the prompt goes to the creative service you connected, such as Higgsfield. The company that makes Konsel never receives your conversations.

Can the AIs change my files?

The AIs can find and read files on your Mac. They cannot change or delete them.

Why does the sign-in page say Claude Code, Codex and so on?

Konsel gets answers through each company's official Mac program: Claude Code from Anthropic for Claude, Codex from OpenAI for ChatGPT, Grok Build from xAI, GitHub Copilot CLI, Cursor CLI and Kimi Code CLI. You sign in with the accounts you already use for each AI.

What usage data does Konsel send?

Once a day: the app version, the macOS version, the names of the connected AIs, how many messages you sent and answers you got, and at which step and of what type any errors were. Never your conversations, name, email or file paths. It goes to PostHog's servers in Germany. You choose whether to send it when you first open the app, and can change it later in Settings on the My screen.

How do I report a problem?

Use "Send feedback" below the chat list or on the My screen. In a chat, say "Tell them this is annoying" and the AI opens the feedback window with a draft. You can also press "Report this problem" on an error message. Either way, you press Send yourself.
