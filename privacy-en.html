Konsel Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 1, 2026

Posted: November 24, 2026

LuluLabs Inc. ("we") handles personal information in the Konsel Mac app and the Konsel download web page (the "Service") as described in this policy.

1. Key principles

  • Konsel runs on your Mac. Your conversations, memory and sign-in details are stored on your Mac and are not sent to our servers.
  • There is no Konsel account. We never receive or store your passwords. You sign in to each AI on that company's own sign-in page.
  • We receive only usage data to improve the app, feedback you choose to send, and visits to the web page.

2.3 Visits to the download web page

IP addresses are not stored. Visits are not linked to each other, so they cannot reveal your interests, and they are not used for advertising. You can block this with your browser's tracking protection without affecting the page.

2.4 Update checks

When it starts and every 6 hours, the app asks GitHub (GitHub, Inc., United States) for the latest version of Konsel and whether the current version can still be used. We use this to notify you in the app when the beta ends or when we stop a version that has a serious problem. The request contains no personal information, but as with any internet request GitHub receives your IP address. We do not receive any record of these requests.

For an AI to answer or to make an image, the conversation is sent to the company that runs that AI. You send it, directly from your Mac, only to the AIs you connect. We do not receive it or pass it on. Each company processes what it receives under its own privacy policy, which also states where it processes data and for how long.

You can remove these connections with "Remove from other apps" on the My screen.

You can stop sending to an AI or a generation service by disconnecting it on the My screen.

5. Your choices and rights

  • On the My screen you can disconnect AIs, sign out, delete saved memory, turn off usage statistics, and remove Konsel from other apps.
  • Data on your Mac is in the app data folder (~/Library/Application Support/Konsel), ~/Konsel and ~/Library/Logs/Konsel. Deleting these folders deletes all of it. Before deleting the app, use "Remove from other apps" on the My screen to remove Konsel from other AI apps and the generation service sign-ins from the Keychain.
  • You have the right to access, correct and delete the information we hold, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing before the withdrawal.
  • Send requests to the privacy email below. We reply within one month. Usage data is tied only to a random install ID, so include the install number shown on the My screen to help us find it.
  • If you are in the EU or EEA, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act.

6. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for users aged 16 and over (14 and over in the Republic of Korea) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16, and we delete it without delay if we learn that we have.

7. Deletion

We delete information without delay when its retention period ends, in a way that cannot be recovered. You can delete information stored on your Mac yourself in the app or by deleting the app data folder.

8. Security measures

  • The AIs act only on your messages. They edit a copy of the folder linked to a chat, and the original changes only when you press "Apply to my folder" or when a command you allowed on a permission card changes it.
  • The AIs cannot read secret files (SSH keys, other apps' sign-in details), and they ask for permission before connecting to internet addresses other than their own company's servers or reading a web page.
  • Sign-in details for generation services are kept in the macOS Keychain and never passed to the AIs. Tools that look like payment, publishing or deletion are blocked, and any tool that is not read-only asks for your permission the first time it is used for each service in a chat.
  • Usage data and feedback are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Access to the information we receive is limited to the staff who need it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes on this page or in the app 7 days before they take effect, or 30 days before for material changes to your rights.
